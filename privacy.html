Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Tambola Caller Android app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) uses and why. The app is made by Tambola Caller. If you have questions, email namish.malhotra.2013@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t collect your name, phone number, contacts or files.
  • Your games, settings, history and custom calls are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads (see below).

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it works the way you left it: the current game and the numbers called, your settings (theme, voice, language, speed), your game history, custom call phrases, Premium status and any ad-free time you’ve earned. This stays on your device, isn’t sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have Premium or ad-free time, the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device type, operating system, app version and language,
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps), and crash or diagnostic data from the ads SDK.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you’ll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings under Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by phone).

Voice (text-to-speech)

Numbers are spoken using your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine. If you pick an “online” voice, your phone’s speech engine (usually Google’s) may send the text being spoken, such as “Two little ducks, 22”, to its servers to produce the audio, under that provider’s own privacy policy. Offline voices work entirely on your device.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK as described above.
  • Vibration: to buzz when a number is called (you can turn this off).
  • Keep screen awake: so the screen doesn’t turn off during a game.

Premium and payments

Premium features are unlocked on your device. The app does not currently process payments. If paid Premium is added later, payments will be handled by the app store or payment provider, and this policy will be updated.

Children

The app is a general-audience game tool and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we’ll help.

Data sharing and selling

We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t share it with anyone, because we don’t receive it. Information collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s policies linked above.

Security and retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
